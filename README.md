# 🎬 My Favorite Series: *Queen of Tears*

![Queen of Tears Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/2/22/Queen_of_Tears_poster.jpg)

## 🌸 Overview
**Queen of Tears** is a South Korean romantic drama series that tells the story of a married couple overcoming challenges in their relationship while rediscovering love.  
It beautifully blends *romance*, *comedy*, and *emotional storytelling*—a true masterpiece of K-drama writing.
